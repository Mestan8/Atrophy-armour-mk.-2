## Extraction, tables, figures, and visual checks

**Text-accessible:** All 8 PDFs and all 89 pages; no OCR was needed. Article titles/authors/abstracts were text-extractable except that the Gopalakrishnan abstract PDF omits its article citation. Metadata extraction itself is not dependable for identity in the English and Fitts PDFs and is blank/incomplete for the one-page abstract.

**Table/figure access:** Result pages contain selectable text and rendered figures/tables. PyMuPDF detected table-like regions in 7 PDFs; the one-page abstract has none. This does not mean cell structure was reliably recovered. Representative rendered pages were inspected for each document: English 4-5; Scott 2-5; Trappe 3-6; Sprint 2-4 and 6; LeBlanc 4-5; Gopalakrishnan 1; Fitts 6-7; Greene 17. They are legible at rendered resolution. These spot checks do **not** verify every table cell or every figure value.

Programmatic `find_tables().extract()` output was often unsuitable for importing numbers without checking the rendered source: table columns/cells were merged or split in English, Scott, Trappe, LeBlanc, Fitts, and Greene examples; minus signs, ± signs, decimal points, and superscripts were inconsistently represented. Sprint's sampled tables were more structured, but still require value/context checks. Rendered English/Fitts/Sprint/Greene tables show units, decimals, percentage signs, and variability fields, but the text-only result is not a reliable transcription. The one-page 2010 abstract text and image both show a suspicious calf-volume range printed/extracted as “-10 to 16%”; resolve against the full source before using it.

## Study Library integration path and remaining blockers

- Study records are defined as static dictionaries in `study_library.py`'s `STUDIES` tuple and seeded into SQLite with `INSERT OR IGNORE`; there is no study-record import UI. The current records are English and Fitts only.
- A record stores `source_file` (filename). The UI displays `data/references/{source_file}`; it does not open or parse the PDF dynamically. The eight PDFs can therefore be referenced by filename once correct study metadata is added.
- Measurement entry is separate. The app allows manually entered measurement records and records page, table/figure, context, units, timepoint and verification status. The app also seeds curated verified observations for the existing English/Fitts scenario evidence on startup; it does not automatically extract or import values from the PDFs.
- Measurement `verification_status` is tracked (`unverified` / `source_verified`), but no PDF extraction status or study-level source-verification state is tracked. Adding a study record does not mean its observations are verified or used by the model.
- Idempotent seeding avoids duplicates only when the same primary-key study ID is reused. There is no unique DOI/title/source-file constraint or bulk importer; use stable IDs and check DOI/source filename before adding records. The root-level English PDF is a byte-identical duplicate, but the Study Library caption points to the copy in `data/references/`.

**Recommended next phase:** add metadata for the six new studies using validated titles/DOIs and stable IDs; keep the Gopalakrishnan abstract explicitly marked as incomplete until its full article is obtained; obtain Scott/Sprint supplements if their supplemental results are needed. Then select observations manually, record their exact source page/table/figure, wording, unit, baseline/timepoint, sample-size meaning, and uncertainty, and reconcile every value against rendered source pages before marking it `source_verified`. Assess biological level, population, countermeasure and endpoint compatibility separately before any value is curated as a model input. Do not import these documents' values during this diagnostic.
